What to include

Helpful details for a first email.

  • Your systems. ERP, CRM, HR and banking, and the FP&A platform or Power BI setup you use or are considering.
  • Your structure. How many entities and currencies you report on, and whether intercompany activity is involved.
  • Your goal. The reports you need, or what isn't working in your current setup.
  • Your timing. Any close, audit or board date you are working toward.

Please don't send passwords, account credentials or client financial data by email. We arrange secure, approved access only once an engagement is agreed.
