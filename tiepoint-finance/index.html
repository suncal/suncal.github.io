01 / The problem

The software is rarely the hard part. The data under it is.

Most finance teams already own good tools. What slows them down is the plumbing between those tools, and the doubt that creeps in when two reports disagree.

× 01

Month-end runs on exports

Someone downloads from the ERP, pastes into a workbook and rebuilds the same report every month. One missed row and the numbers drift.

× 02

The dashboard and the ledger disagree

When a report doesn't match the books, leadership stops trusting the report, and the team goes back to spreadsheets.

× 03

Consolidation lives in a workbook

Several entities, several currencies and intercompany activity, all stitched together by hand under deadline pressure.

× 04

The rollout stalled halfway

The FP&A platform was bought, a few connectors went in, and the project lost momentum before reports were ready to use.

05 / Tie-out checks

Five checks. No report ships until all five pass.

This is the standard behind the name. We run the same five checks on every engagement, document the results and share them with you before release.

Every GL account is mapped exactly once

No account left unmapped, none counted twice. Unmapped balances are the quietest way a report goes wrong.
