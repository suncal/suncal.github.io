About

Named for the point where it all agrees.

In surveying, a tie point is a fixed reference that every measurement is checked against. For finance teams, that reference is the general ledger. We build systems that keep coming back to it.

Leadership
PC

Priyankar Chakraborty

Managing Member, P CHAK Consulting LLC

Priyankar leads TiePoint Finance engagements. He brings more than 18 years in IT, including over five years as an AWS Cloud Architect, and a long background in EDI and system integrations: the work of getting separate systems to exchange data accurately and on schedule.

That integration discipline is the core of TiePoint Finance. Finance reporting problems are very often integration problems in disguise: a feed that drops records, a mapping that double counts, a currency handled two different ways. Priyankar's focus is on finding those issues early and proving they are fixed.

He scopes each engagement, leads delivery and stays responsible for the tie-out results you sign off on.

How we work.

Data security

Your data stays where you put it.

Finance data is sensitive. These rules apply on every engagement.

  • Your systems only. We work inside systems you approve, not on copies kept elsewhere.
  • MFA on every account. Access we use is protected with multi-factor authentication.
  • Least privilege. Read-only access wherever the work allows it, and no broader permissions than a task requires.
  • Access removed at the end. When the engagement closes, our accounts and credentials are removed, and we confirm it in writing.
  • Agreements first. Everyone on the project signs an NDA and a non-solicitation agreement before any client data is shared.
