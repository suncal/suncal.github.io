Context

The books close in Business Central. Then the copying starts.

Business Central is a capable ERP for growing companies, and its dimensions give finance teams plenty of detail to report on. Getting that detail into a reliable monthly view, across several companies and currencies, is where many teams lose time.

We build the reporting layer: a Power BI model or FP&A connection based on Business Central data, with dimensions mapped, companies consolidated and intercompany eliminated. Before handover, everything ties to the Business Central trial balance.

Technical considerations

What matters in a Business Central project.

The specifics we look at early, because they decide how clean the data will be. Product details change, so we confirm them against the vendor's current documentation during discovery.

Companies

Each Business Central company keeps its own ledger. Group reporting combines them, so the chart of accounts and dimensions need to line up across companies first.

Dimensions

Business Central supports global and shortcut dimensions for analysis. We confirm which dimensions are mandatory and how they map into reporting.

Consolidation

Business Central includes a consolidation feature based on business units. We decide with your controller whether to consolidate there, in an FP&A tool, or in the reporting model.

Power BI

Microsoft provides a Business Central connector for Power BI. We build a finance model on it, with measures defined once and reconciled to the ledger.

Pitfalls

Common problems, and how we avoid them.

These come up in Business Central projects regardless of who runs them.

Dimension values that differ by company

We align dimension codes before building group reports.

Measures defined in each report

We define measures once in the model so every page agrees.

Consolidating twice

We pick one place for consolidation logic and document it.

Before release

Every Business Central report passes five checks.

The same tie-out standard applies on every platform, for every entity and month in scope.

CheckWhy it mattersPass condition
  1. 01

    Every GL account is mapped exactly once

    Reports only add up if each ledger account lands in one reporting line, once.

    unmapped = 0
  2. 02

    Totals tie to the trial balance

    The trial balance is the agreed source of truth, so every entity and every month is compared against it.

    variance = 0.00
  3. 03

    Intercompany nets to zero

    A group should not report trading with itself. After eliminations, intercompany balances must cancel.

    IC net = 0.00
  4. 04

    Correct FX rates and periods

    Translation uses different rate types for different balances, and each month needs its own rates.

    rates = policy
  5. 05

    Large movements are explained

    A number can tie to the ledger and still mislead. Big swings need a reason before a board sees them.

    unexplained = 0

FAQ

Business Central questions, answered plainly.

Is TiePoint Finance a Microsoft partner?

No. We are independent and have no affiliation with Microsoft. Our work sits on the finance side of Business Central: reporting, integration and consolidation.

Will you build Power BI reporting on top of Business Central?

Yes. We design the model, build the report pages and prove the totals against the Business Central ledger before your team starts using them.

We run several companies in Business Central. Can you consolidate them?

Yes. We set up the company hierarchy, intercompany matching and eliminations, and FX rules so group reports are consistent.

Can Business Central feed an FP&A platform?

Yes. A scheduled feed into Datarails, Vena, Cube or a similar tool means budgets and forecasts start from current ledger figures.

Do you customise Business Central itself?

Our work is mainly reporting, integration and consolidation. If discovery shows a need for deeper ERP changes, we'll say so plainly.
