Context

Spreadsheet freedom needs a solid centre.

Cube's idea is simple: your team keeps working in spreadsheets while the numbers come from a single governed source. That only pays off if the source is structured well. Dimensions that don't match how the business reports, or accounts that map inconsistently, make every connected sheet harder to trust.

We design that centre with you. We connect your systems, build dimensions and hierarchies around how you actually report, map accounts and currencies, and tie actuals to your general ledger before the planning models go live.
