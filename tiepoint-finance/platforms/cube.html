Context

Spreadsheet freedom needs a solid center.

Cube's idea is simple: your team keeps working in spreadsheets while the numbers come from a single governed source. That only pays off if the source is structured well. Dimensions that don't match how the business reports, or accounts that map inconsistently, make every connected sheet harder to trust.

We design that center with you. We connect your systems, build dimensions and hierarchies around how you actually report, map accounts and currencies, and tie actuals to your general ledger before the planning models go live.

Technical considerations

What matters in a Cube project.

The specifics we look at early, because they decide how clean the data will be. Product details change, so we confirm them against the vendor's current documentation during discovery.

Spreadsheet add-ins

Cube connects Excel and Google Sheets to a central data source through add-ins, so planners keep their own spreadsheets.

Dimensions and hierarchies

The central structure decides how every connected sheet slices data. We design it around how you report, not around one source system.

Actuals loading

Actuals from your ERP land in the central source on a schedule. We map them once there, rather than inside each sheet.

Model hygiene

We keep formulas and inputs separated so a refresh never overwrites a planner's assumptions.

Pitfalls

Common problems, and how we avoid them.

These come up in Cube projects regardless of who runs them.

Recreating every legacy tab

We rebuild only what is used and retire the rest.

Mapping inside sheets

Mapping lives in the central source so every sheet agrees.

Unclear version control

We agree how budget, forecast and scenarios are named and locked.

Before release

Every Cube report passes five checks.

The same tie-out standard applies on every platform, for every entity and month in scope.

CheckWhy it mattersPass condition
  1. 01

    Every GL account is mapped exactly once

    Reports only add up if each ledger account lands in one reporting line, once.

    unmapped = 0
  2. 02

    Totals tie to the trial balance

    The trial balance is the agreed source of truth, so every entity and every month is compared against it.

    variance = 0.00
  3. 03

    Intercompany nets to zero

    A group should not report trading with itself. After eliminations, intercompany balances must cancel.

    IC net = 0.00
  4. 04

    Correct FX rates and periods

    Translation uses different rate types for different balances, and each month needs its own rates.

    rates = policy
  5. 05

    Large movements are explained

    A number can tie to the ledger and still mislead. Big swings need a reason before a board sees them.

    unexplained = 0
