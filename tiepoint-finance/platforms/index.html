Choosing a platform

Not sure which tool fits?

  • Start from your reporting, not the feature list. The right platform is the one that fits how your team closes, plans and presents.
  • Count your entities and currencies. Consolidation needs shape the choice more than most demos suggest.
  • Decide where people will work. Excel-first teams, Sheets users and teams wanting a dedicated interface lean toward different tools.
  • Check your sources early. Confirm how each ERP, CRM and HR system will connect before you sign.
  • Use discovery to compare. We can assess the options against your systems and give a straight recommendation, since we sell no licences.
