Context

The ledger is in NetSuite. The reporting often isn't.

Plenty of finance teams run NetSuite well for transactions and still build their management reports in spreadsheets. Saved searches multiply, exports get pasted into workbooks, and consolidated numbers depend on someone remembering the right steps.

We work on the finance side: integrations, subsidiary and intercompany setup, reporting, and a dependable actuals feed into Datarails, Cube, Vena, Planful, Adaptive Planning or Power BI. Everything we build is tied back to the NetSuite trial balance before it is released.

Independent consultancy. TiePoint Finance is not affiliated with, endorsed by or a partner of the vendor of NetSuite. We work for your finance team.

Do you partner with Oracle NetSuite?

We don't. TiePoint Finance is independent and earns nothing from licence sales, so our recommendations are shaped only by your requirements.

Do you do full NetSuite implementations?

Our scope is the finance layer: configuration that affects reporting, integrations, consolidation, migration and planning-tool feeds. Discovery tells us both whether that matches what you need.

We went live years ago. Can you still help?

Yes. Teams that have run NetSuite for a while are a natural fit: the ledger is stable, and the gaps are usually in reporting, integrations or group consolidation.

Can NetSuite feed Datarails, Cube or Adaptive Planning?

It can, and that link is a large part of our work. We build the feed, then tie the loaded figures back to NetSuite before anyone plans on them.

What access do you need?

The narrowest role that lets us do the job, usually read-only for design and reporting work, always with MFA. We hand it back when the engagement closes.
