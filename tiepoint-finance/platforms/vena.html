Context

The template is the visible part. The model decides everything.

Vena gives finance teams workflows, approvals and templates in a tool they already know. How useful it becomes depends on the data model under those templates: the dimensions, hierarchies and integration rules that decide where every number lands.

We start there. We design the model with your team, integrate your ERP and other sources, build planning and reporting templates, and set up consolidation with intercompany eliminations. Every report is checked against your ledger before release.

Independent consultancy. TiePoint Finance is not affiliated with, endorsed by or a partner of the vendor of Vena. We work for your finance team.

Does TiePoint Finance partner with Vena?

No. We are independent and not affiliated with Vena. Where Vena's own onboarding team is involved, we coordinate with them on your behalf.

Vena is already live for us. Where would you start?

Yes. We start with a review of the model, templates and integrations, fix whatever causes errors or rework, and then add the reporting you are missing.

How does our ERP data get into Vena?

Yes. We set up the integration so ledger actuals refresh on a schedule rather than through exported files.

Do you build Power BI reports on Vena data?

Yes, where it suits your reporting. We make sure the Power BI figures agree with Vena and with the ledger.

How long does a Vena project take?

Most land in the 6 to 10 week range; the number of entities, sources and templates decides where. The fixed quote from discovery sets it.
