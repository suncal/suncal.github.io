Context

One platform for planning and close still needs clean inputs.

Planful puts budgeting, forecasting, consolidation and reporting under one roof in the cloud. Like any FP&A platform, it reflects the data it is given. Inconsistent mappings or missing entities show up later as reports that need explaining.

We handle the inputs and the proof: integrations from your ERP and other systems, account and entity mapping, model design and consolidation rules, followed by a tie-out to the ledger for recent closed months.

Technical considerations

What matters in a Planful project.

The specifics we look at early, because they decide how clean the data will be. Product details change, so we confirm them against the vendor's current documentation during discovery.

Planning and close in one place

Planful covers planning, consolidation and reporting. We agree early which processes move into it and in what order.

Structure

Entities, accounts and dimensions in Planful need to reflect both legal reporting and management reporting. We design for both.

Consolidation rules

Currency translation and intercompany eliminations are set up as rules and tested against the ledger.

Data loads

Actuals are loaded from your ERP on a schedule and reconciled before reports or plans depend on them.

Pitfalls

Common problems, and how we avoid them.

These come up in Planful projects regardless of who runs them.

Moving every process at once

We sequence: actuals and reporting first, then planning.

Management and legal structures mixed

We keep the two hierarchies distinct.

Eliminations untested

Check 03 runs on every entity pair before release.

Before release

Every Planful report passes five checks.

The same tie-out standard applies on every platform, for every entity and month in scope.

CheckWhy it mattersPass condition
  1. 01

    Every GL account is mapped exactly once

    Reports only add up if each ledger account lands in one reporting line, once.

    unmapped = 0
  2. 02

    Totals tie to the trial balance

    The trial balance is the agreed source of truth, so every entity and every month is compared against it.

    variance = 0.00
  3. 03

    Intercompany nets to zero

    A group should not report trading with itself. After eliminations, intercompany balances must cancel.

    IC net = 0.00
  4. 04

    Correct FX rates and periods

    Translation uses different rate types for different balances, and each month needs its own rates.

    rates = policy
  5. 05

    Large movements are explained

    A number can tie to the ledger and still mislead. Big swings need a reason before a board sees them.

    unexplained = 0

Are you affiliated with Planful?

No. TiePoint Finance is an independent consultancy with no affiliation to Planful.

Can you fix an existing Planful setup?

Yes. We diagnose where the numbers or the model go wrong, correct it, and fill the reporting gaps you have been working around.

Which systems can feed Planful?

Yes. Ledger data comes first, and we bring in CRM, HR or bank sources where the plan or reports depend on them.

Do you help with consolidation in Planful?

Yes. We set up entity hierarchies, FX handling and intercompany eliminations, and check that the group totals tie out.
