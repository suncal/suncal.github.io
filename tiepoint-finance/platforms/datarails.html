Context

Excel stays familiar. The data behind it has to be right.

The appeal of Datarails is that finance teams don't have to give up the workbooks they know. The catch is that those workbooks are only as good as the data mapped into them. If an account lands in the wrong line, or an entity's currency is handled inconsistently, the familiar spreadsheet will show a confident, wrong number.

We focus on that foundation. We connect your sources, design the mapping so every ledger account lands once, set up consolidation and eliminations, and run our five tie-out checks before your models and dashboards go live. If a rollout has stalled, we start by finding out why.

Technical considerations

What matters in a Datarails project.

The specifics we look at early, because they decide how clean the data will be. Product details change, so we confirm them against the vendor's current documentation during discovery.

Excel add-in

Datarails works through an Excel add-in, so models stay in workbooks your team knows. That makes the structure of those workbooks part of the implementation, not an afterthought.

Mapping

Source trial balances are mapped to your reporting structure. We design it so a new account is caught at the next refresh instead of disappearing from totals.

Consolidation

Entities, currencies and intercompany are set up as rules, agreed with your controller, rather than rebuilt in each workbook.

Refresh discipline

We document which workbooks refresh from which data and when, so nobody presents a stale number.

FAQ

Datarails questions, answered plainly.

Are you part of Datarails?

No. TiePoint Finance is an independent consultancy with no affiliation to Datarails. We work for your finance team, and we are happy to work alongside any onboarding Datarails provides.

We already have Datarails. Is it too late to bring you in?

Not at all. A lot of our work starts after the subscription is signed, when connectors, mapping and reports still need finishing. Discovery begins from where you are today.

Can we keep our current Excel models?

Usually, yes. We review them in discovery and reconnect the ones worth keeping to live actuals. Where a model is fragile, we'll say so and suggest a simpler structure.

How do you prove the Datarails numbers are right?

We tie loaded actuals to your trial balance for each entity and month in scope, check intercompany and FX, and document the results before reports go live.

How long does a Datarails project take?

Most of our projects run 6 to 10 weeks. Discovery confirms the timeline and gives you a fixed quote.
