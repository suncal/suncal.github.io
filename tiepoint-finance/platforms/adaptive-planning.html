Context

A driver-based plan is only as good as its actuals.

Driver-based planning lets finance model the business the way it really works: headcount, pricing, volumes and rates. It also means a wrong actual can ripple through every forecast that depends on it.

We build Adaptive Planning models around your real drivers, connect ERP, HR and CRM sources, map accounts and levels, and reconcile actuals to the general ledger. Workforce and revenue plans then start from numbers your controller agrees with.

Technical considerations

What matters in a Adaptive Planning project.

The specifics we look at early, because they decide how clean the data will be. Product details change, so we confirm them against the vendor's current documentation during discovery.

Levels and dimensions

Adaptive Planning organizes plans by levels (your organizational structure) and dimensions. We design both around how budgets are owned.

Versions

Budgets, forecasts and scenarios live in separate versions. We agree naming, locking and which version reports compare against.

Sheet types

Adaptive Planning offers different sheet types, such as standard, cube and modeled sheets. Each suits a different planning job, and we choose per use.

Integration

Actuals and headcount load through Adaptive Planning's integration tools or file loads. We reconcile them to the ledger and the HR system.

Pitfalls

Common problems, and how we avoid them.

These come up in Adaptive Planning projects regardless of who runs them.

Drivers that nobody can explain

We document each driver, its owner and its source.

Too many sheets

We consolidate overlapping sheets and keep the model maintainable.

Unreconciled headcount

Workforce plans start from HR data checked against payroll and the ledger.

Before release

Every Adaptive Planning report passes five checks.

The same tie-out standard applies on every platform, for every entity and month in scope.

CheckWhy it mattersPass condition
  1. 01

    Every GL account is mapped exactly once

    Reports only add up if each ledger account lands in one reporting line, once.

    unmapped = 0
  2. 02

    Totals tie to the trial balance

    The trial balance is the agreed source of truth, so every entity and every month is compared against it.

    variance = 0.00
  3. 03

    Intercompany nets to zero

    A group should not report trading with itself. After eliminations, intercompany balances must cancel.

    IC net = 0.00
  4. 04

    Correct FX rates and periods

    Translation uses different rate types for different balances, and each month needs its own rates.

    rates = policy
  5. 05

    Large movements are explained

    A number can tie to the ledger and still mislead. Big swings need a reason before a board sees them.

    unexplained = 0
