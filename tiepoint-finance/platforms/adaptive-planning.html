Context

A driver-based plan is only as good as its actuals.

Driver-based planning lets finance model the business the way it really works: headcount, pricing, volumes and rates. It also means a wrong actual can ripple through every forecast that depends on it.

We build Adaptive Planning models around your real drivers, connect ERP, HR and CRM sources, map accounts and levels, and reconcile actuals to the general ledger. Workforce and revenue plans then start from numbers your controller agrees with.

Independent consultancy. TiePoint Finance is not affiliated with, endorsed by or a partner of the vendor of Adaptive Planning. We work for your finance team.
