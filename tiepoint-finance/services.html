Every ledger account and dimension member is mapped into a single reporting structure that works across entities.

  • Mapping tables your team can maintain without us

Roll-ups across entities and currencies set up to match how your accounting team already closes.

  • Signed-off results kept for audit reference

Common pitfalls

Where finance systems projects usually go wrong.

None of these are exotic. They are the ordinary reasons a new platform ends up distrusted. Each one has a specific countermeasure in how we work.

Building dashboards before the data ties

It feels like progress, but every later fix changes the numbers people already saw. We prove the data first.

Mapping in a spreadsheet nobody owns

A mapping table without an owner drifts within a quarter. We assign ownership and a rule for new accounts.

Treating consolidation as a report

Eliminations and FX translation are accounting logic. We agree them with your controller before they are built.

Loading only the current month

One clean month proves little. We reconcile several recent closed months to catch timing and cut-off issues.

Skipping the runbook

When the person who built it leaves, so does the knowledge. Handover includes written steps and training.

Over-broad system access

Admin access is rarely needed. We work read-only wherever possible and remove access at the end.

Quality gate

The Five Tie-out Checks.

Run on every engagement, for every entity and month in scope. Results are shared with you before release.

CheckWhy it matters and what it catchesPass condition
  1. 01

    Every GL account is mapped exactly once

    Reports only add up if each ledger account lands in one reporting line, once.

    CatchesNew accounts opened after go-live that silently drop out of totals, and accounts assigned to two lines that double count.

    unmapped = 0
  2. 02

    Totals tie to the trial balance

    The trial balance is the agreed source of truth, so every entity and every month is compared against it.

    CatchesPartial or failed loads, cut-off timing differences, and late journals that were posted but never refreshed.

    variance = 0.00
  3. 03

    Intercompany nets to zero

    A group should not report trading with itself. After eliminations, intercompany balances must cancel.

    CatchesOne-sided postings, mismatched counterparty codes, and FX differences left on intercompany balances.

    IC net = 0.00
  4. 04

    Correct FX rates and periods

    Translation uses different rate types for different balances, and each month needs its own rates.

    CatchesThe wrong rate type, a rate from the wrong month, a missing currency, or a translation difference that was never booked.

    rates = policy
  5. 05

    Large movements are explained

    A number can tie to the ledger and still mislead. Big swings need a reason before a board sees them.

    CatchesMisclassified postings, reversals in the wrong period, one-off items, and mapping changes that moved history.

    unexplained = 0
