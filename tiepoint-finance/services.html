What we deliver

Six services, one accountable team.

Take the full set or only what you are missing. Discovery confirms which pieces you actually need.

S.01

Source connections

We connect each system using native connectors, APIs or scheduled file feeds, chosen for reliability rather than novelty.

  • Read-only, least-privilege service accounts
  • Scheduled refresh with failure alerts where the platform supports it
  • Documented credentials ownership: yours, not ours
S.02

Chart of accounts mapping

Every ledger account and dimension mapped into a single reporting structure that works across entities.

  • Mapping tables your team can maintain
  • Rules for new accounts so nothing slips through
  • Department, class, location and project dimensions
S.03

Consolidation

Roll-ups across entities and currencies set up to match how your accounting team already closes.

  • Intercompany matching and eliminations
  • Average and period-end FX rates by policy
  • Ownership and reporting hierarchies
S.04

Reconciliation to the general ledger

We load recent closed months and prove them against the trial balance before any report is built on top.

  • Entity-by-month tie-out workbook
  • Every difference traced to a root cause
  • Signed-off results kept for audit reference
S.05

Dashboards and board packs

Reports designed with the people who read them, in your FP&A platform or in Power BI.

  • P&L and budget vs actual with drill-down
  • Cash position and cash flow
  • KPI scorecards and board reporting packs
S.06

Training and runbooks

Your team should be able to run month-end without us. We make sure of that before we leave.

  • Role-based training sessions
  • A written month-end runbook
  • Troubleshooting notes for common failures

Engagement options

Pick the depth of help you need.

Contracts are with P CHAK Consulting LLC, our parent company.

  • Paid Discovery. One to two weeks, fixed fee. You receive a mapping plan, dashboard specifications and a fixed implementation quote. The documents are yours whether or not you continue with us.
  • Fixed-scope Implementation. The build agreed in discovery, at the quoted price. Connect and map, validate and reconcile, then dashboards and handover, with your sign-off at each gate.
  • Ongoing Support. An optional monthly arrangement after go-live: month-end health checks, fixes when a source system changes, new entities or accounts mapped, and new reports as your needs grow.
  • Data security throughout. We work only in systems you approve, with MFA and read-only or least-privilege access. Access is removed at the end. Everyone on the project signs an NDA and a non-solicitation agreement before any client data is shared.
Questions

Before you book.

Are you a reseller or partner of these vendors?

No. TiePoint Finance is an independent consultancy. We don't sell licences and we are not affiliated with any of the software vendors named on this site. That lets us recommend what fits your team.

We haven't chosen an FP&A platform yet. Can you help?

Yes. Discovery can compare the platforms you are considering against your systems, entity structure and reporting needs. We'll tell you plainly where each one fits and where it doesn't.

What do you need from our team?

A finance owner who can answer mapping questions and approve each gate, plus someone who can grant system access. We keep the time asked of your team as small as we can.

Can you work alongside the vendor's onboarding team?

Yes. Many platforms include their own onboarding. We fill the gaps around it: source connections, mapping, consolidation logic and reconciliation.

How is pricing set?

Discovery is a fixed fee. Implementation is a fixed quote based on what discovery finds. Ongoing support is a monthly arrangement. We share numbers by email once we understand your setup.
