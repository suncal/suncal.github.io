Glossary

The vocabulary of numbers that agree.

36 terms used across finance systems projects, defined in plain English. Our field guides link here whenever one of these terms appears.

ABCDEFGHIKLMPRSTV
Actuals
Recorded results for a closed period, taken from the general ledger, as opposed to plans or forecasts.
Actuals vs plan
A comparison of recorded results against the budget or forecast for the same period, usually shown with the variance and its direction.
Average rate
An exchange rate averaged over a period. It is commonly used to translate income and expenses, which occur throughout the period.
Board pack
The standard set of reports prepared for a board meeting, usually covering results, cash, KPIs and commentary.
Budget
The approved financial plan for a period, set before the period starts.
Chart of accounts
The list of ledger accounts an entity posts to, organized by type such as assets, liabilities, equity, revenue and expenses.
Close calendar
The schedule of tasks and owners for closing the books each month, with the day each task is due.
Closing rate
The exchange rate at the reporting date. It is commonly used to translate assets and liabilities.
Consolidation
Combining the results of several entities into one set of group figures, after currency translation and intercompany eliminations.
CTA (cumulative translation adjustment)
The difference that arises when an entity's results and balances are translated at different rates. It is recorded in equity rather than in profit or loss.
Dimension
An attribute attached to a transaction for analysis, such as department, location, product or project. Also called a segment.
Driver-based planning
Building a plan from operational drivers such as headcount, price and volume, so that changing a driver updates the financial outcome.
Elimination
An entry made during consolidation to remove intercompany balances and transactions, so the group does not report trading with itself.
Entity
A separate reporting unit, usually a legal company, with its own ledger.
ERP
Enterprise resource planning system. For finance, the system that holds the general ledger and related subledgers.
Forecast
An updated view of expected results, usually revised during the year as actuals come in.
FP&A
Financial planning and analysis: the team and process responsible for budgets, forecasts, management reporting and analysis.
Functional currency
The currency of the primary economic environment an entity operates in. Its books are kept in this currency.
General ledger (GL)
The complete record of an entity's accounts and the postings to them. It is the basis for the trial balance and financial statements.
Global capability center
A center owned by a company that performs functions such as finance, reporting or technology for the wider group, often serving several countries..
Hierarchy
A parent and child structure that rolls detailed items into summaries, such as accounts into P&L lines or entities into regions.
Historical rate
The exchange rate at the date of the original transaction. It is commonly used for equity balances.
Intercompany
Transactions or balances between entities in the same group, such as sales, recharges or loans.
KPI
Key performance indicator: a defined measure used to track performance, such as gross margin or days sales outstanding.
Least privilege
Giving a user only the access a task requires, and no more.
Mapping table
A table that assigns each source account or dimension value to its place in the reporting structure.
Presentation currency
The currency the group reports in. Entities with other functional currencies are translated into it.
Reconciliation
Comparing two sets of figures that should agree, then explaining and correcting any differences.
Rolling forecast
A forecast that always looks a fixed number of months ahead, extended each period instead of ending at year-end.
Runbook
Written, step-by-step instructions for running a recurring process, such as a month-end data refresh.
Shared service center
A central team that performs common processes, such as accounting, close or reporting, for several entities in a group.
Subledger
A detailed ledger for one area, such as receivables or fixed assets, that summarises into the general ledger.
Tie-out
Proving that one set of figures agrees exactly with another, such as reporting totals with the trial balance.
Trial balance
A list of every ledger account with its balance at a date. Total debits equal total credits.
Variance
The difference between two figures, such as actual and budget. It is described as favorable or unfavorable.
Version
A named copy of plan data, such as budget, forecast or a scenario, kept separate so they can be compared.

Definitions are general descriptions of common practice, not accounting standards or advice. Your auditors and the applicable accounting framework decide how a specific balance is treated.
