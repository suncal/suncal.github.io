Flagship

Trust is the bottleneck for AI agents. We build the layer that earns it.

Companies want AI agents doing real work, but can't yet prove which ones are safe to let act. Our flagship answers that with evidence, not promises.

Flagship · AI-agent oversight

Tacit

The verified work layer. Tacit grades every AI agent a company runs against what its own people actually do, then lets the proven ones work, step by step.

  • Trust ladder: an agent shadows, then proposes, then acts on its own
  • A full audit trail behind every decision
  • Evidence for the EU AI Act
  • Self-hosted and MIT-licensed
